# webDevMatt — brand files

The WDM mark: **W** and **M** in ink (or white on dark), **D** in brushed silver. The M sits at the back, the D in front of it, and the W in front of the D.

## logo/
| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `WDM_on-light.svg` / `.png` | The mark on white or light backgrounds — CV, documents |
| `WDM_on-dark.svg` / `.png` | The mark on black or dark backgrounds — slides, banners |

Use the **SVG** in Canva, Figma or print (sharp at any size); the PNGs are transparent.

## letters/
The W, D and M on their own (SVG + transparent PNG, all the same height) in black, white and — for the D — silver. For rearranging or animating the mark.

## social/
| File | Size | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `GitHub_avatar_1000x1000.png` | 1000×1000 | GitHub, any square profile picture (safe for circle crops) |
| `LinkedIn_banner_1584x396.png` | 1584×396 | LinkedIn background banner (content kept clear of the profile photo) |

**Fonts** — Sora ExtraBold for the webDevMatt wordmark · JetBrains Mono for labels
